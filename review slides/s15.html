Why cross-attention helps

Handwriting captures

Fine motor control degradation (micrographia, tremor cues).

Speech captures

Voice quality degradation (dysarthria, hypophonia-related cues).

Cross-modal interaction

Bidirectional attention allows each modality to query the other, learning which handwriting patterns correlate with specific speech characteristics.

This design supports robust multimodal screening beyond what each modality achieves alone.
