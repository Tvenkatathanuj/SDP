Key Achievements & Future Work

Senior Design Project

Multi-modal approach addresses critical limitations in existing PD detection systems by synergistically combining handwriting (EfficientNet-B4 + SPP + CBAM) and speech (tri-path architecture) analysis for comprehensive symptom coverage

Attention-based late fusion adaptively weights modalities per patient, ensuring robustness across diverse symptom presentations and providing superior accuracy compared to single-modality approaches

SMOTE effectively addresses severe class imbalance (5.7:1 ratio), generating 332 synthetic Parkinson's samples to prevent majority-class bias while maintaining high PD detection sensitivity

LOPO cross-validation ensures genuine patient-independent generalization with robust performance on unseen patients, simulating real-world clinical deployment scenarios

Accessible, non-invasive pipeline using smartphone camera and microphone enables clinical screening, home monitoring, and deployment in resource-limited settings without specialized equipment

Next steps: Extended datasets, prospective clinical trials, explainability enhancements (attention visualization), mobile app development, and integration with clinical decision support systems
