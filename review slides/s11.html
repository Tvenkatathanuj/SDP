Data Sources & Feature Extraction

Handwriting Dataset

NewHandPD + PaHaW

Dataset Statistics

Total: 204 images

Training: 144 images (72 Healthy, 72 PD)
Testing: 60 images (30 Healthy, 30 PD)
Types: Spiral & Wave patterns

Format & Preprocessing

Format: PNG images (grayscale/RGB)
Resolution: Variable, resized to 224×224
Augmentation: Geometric + intensity transforms

Extracted Features

Tremor intensity and frequency patterns
Line smoothness and thickness variations
Drawing speed (estimated from strokes)

Speech Audio Dataset

Custom Denoised Recordings

Dataset Statistics

Total: ~320 WAV files (16 kHz)

Parkinson's: 129 recordings
Healthy Controls: ~190 recordings
Duration: 5-second segments (standardized)

Speech Tasks

Sustained vowel phonation (/a/, /e/, /i/, /o/, /u/)
Reading passages (standard texts)
Spontaneous speech (conversations)

Acoustic Features

Mel-Spectrograms (128 bins) MFCC Stats (80 dims)

14 Clinical Markers:

Pitch, Jitter, Shimmer, HNR
Formants (F1-F3), Spectral, Energy features
