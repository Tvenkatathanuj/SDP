Caption

The CMAFN aligns handwriting and speech through bidirectional Transformer cross-attention (Handwriting → Speech and Speech → Handwriting). Fused representations pass through a Gated Multimodal Unit (GMU), then a final classifier, achieving 96.9% accuracy and 0.999 AUC-ROC.

Explicit cross-modal interaction improves over unimodal baselines by learning complementary biomarkers.

What happens in this model

Encode handwriting

EfficientNet backbone produces a compact visual representation.

Encode speech

Pretrained XLS-R features capture voice quality and articulation cues.

Cross-attend + gate

Each modality attends to the other; GMU balances contributions before classification.
