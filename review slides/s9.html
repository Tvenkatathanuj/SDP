Four complementary pathways—XLS-R (self-supervised) + BiLSTM, Mel-spectrogram CNN, MFCC, and Praat acoustic measures—are fused using multi-head cross-attention. Predictions from deep learning and classical ML are stacked with Ridge Logistic Regression, achieving ~91.7% accuracy.
