What this work addresses
Challenge
PD screening can be subjective and costly; early-access tools are limited.

Deployment

Reported study metrics: handwriting (93.6%), speech (91.7%), fusion (96.9%).
Why multimodal?
Handwriting
Captures tremor patterns and fine-motor degradation (e.g., micrographia).
Speech
Reveals dysarthria / hypophonia via embeddings + acoustic measures.
CMAFN fusion
Cross-attention learns complementary signals across modalities.
