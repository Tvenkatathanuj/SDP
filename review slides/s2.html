Abstract

Multi-Modal PD Detection

Senior Design Project

Novel multi-modal deep learning system for early Parkinson's Disease detection combining handwriting analysis and speech patterns. PD is a progressive neurodegenerative disorder affecting millions worldwide, characterized by motor and non-motor symptoms including tremors, rigidity, bradykinesia, and speech impairments.

Handwriting Analysis: EfficientNet-B4 with Spatial Pyramid Pooling (SPP) and Convolutional Block Attention Module (CBAM) analyzes spiral and wave patterns to detect motor symptoms like tremor and micrographia.

Speech Analysis: Tri-path architecture combining mel-spectrograms (CNN), MFCC features (MLP), and clinical acoustic markers (jitter, shimmer, HNR, formants - MLP) to identify dysarthria and voice quality degradation.

SMOTE Class Balancing: Addresses severe class imbalance (5.7:1 HC:PD ratio) using SMOTE to generate 332 synthetic Parkinson's samples, ensuring balanced training and preventing majority-class bias.

Attention-Based Fusion: Late fusion mechanism with attention adaptively weights features from both modalities, achieving superior accuracy and robustness compared to single-modality approaches.

LOPO Cross-Validation: Leave-One-Patient-Out validation ensures generalization across diverse patient populations, providing a non-invasive, accessible, and cost-effective solution for clinical screening and remote monitoring.
