Dual-pathway ensemble for PD/Healthy classification

Fig. A

Caption

Dual-pathway ensemble combines an MLP over 16 engineered spatial biomarkers with a CNN (EfficientNet-B0) enhanced by attention. A Logistic Regression meta-learner fuses both predictions, achieving ~93.6% accuracy.

Input drawings: spiral/wave; features capture motor control degradation (micrographia/tremor cues).

What happens in this module

Path 1 — Biomarkers → MLP

Captures nonlinear relationships among engineered features.

Path 2 — Image → CNN

EfficientNet-B0 learns spatial patterns directly from drawings.

Fusion — Meta Learner

Logistic regression stacks predictions to improve robustness.

Why it matters

Handwriting provides a portable, non-invasive motor biomarker—useful alone and even stronger when fused with speech in CMAFN.
