Limitations of Existing Systems

Critical Gaps in Current Approaches

Single-modality limitations: Most systems rely on either handwriting OR speech alone, missing complementary diagnostic information. Typical accuracy 86–94%, failing to capture full spectrum of PD symptoms

Poor patient-level generalization: Models show high accuracy on specific datasets but fail on new patients. Simple train-test splits inflate metrics; lack rigorous LOPO validation for real-world deployment

Ineffective fusion in multi-modal work: Simple concatenation or early fusion ignores complex inter-modal relationships. Lack attention mechanisms to adaptively weight modalities based on per-patient reliability

Small, imbalanced datasets: Most studies use <500 samples with severe class imbalance (3:1 to 5.7:1 HC:PD). Traditional weighted loss insufficient; need advanced oversampling (SMOTE) to prevent majority-class bias

Limited clinical interpretability: Deep learning "black boxes" without meaningful explanations. Healthcare professionals require understanding of which features (tremor, pitch variation) drive predictions for trust and validation
