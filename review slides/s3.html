Core challenges in PD screening
Subjective clinical assessment

High-cost confirmatory tests

Late clinical onset
Motor symptoms appear after substantial neuron loss, shrinking early intervention time.
Need scalable, low-cost screening
Non-invasive, easy capture on consumer hardware, suitable for mass and remote screening.
