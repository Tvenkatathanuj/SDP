Background & Significance

Parkinson's Disease Challenges

Global Impact: Parkinson's Disease is the second most common neurodegenerative disorder globally, affecting over 10 million people with increasing prevalence due to aging populations.

Diagnostic Challenge: Early and accurate diagnosis remains challenging as symptoms often manifest subtly in initial stages, and clinical assessment relies heavily on subjective neurological examinations.

Motor Symptoms: Tremor, rigidity, and bradykinesia significantly impact handwriting, causing micrographia (small, cramped writing) and irregular spiral/wave drawings.

Speech Impairments: Reduced loudness, monotone voice, and imprecise articulation affect 70-90% of PD patients, providing valuable diagnostic biomarkers.

Deep Learning Opportunity: Recent advances in deep learning and computer vision enable automated analysis of these motor and vocal biomarkers, offering objective, quantitative assessment tools that outperform traditional single-modality methods.

Project Goal: Develop a state-of-the-art, accessible screening tool to assist clinicians in early PD detection and monitor disease progression over time using complementary handwriting and speech analysis.
