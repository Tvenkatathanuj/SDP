❔ How to Use

🎯 Goal: This tool screens for early Parkinson's Disease signs by analyzing handwriting patterns. It detects tremor, slowness, and irregularities in your drawing.


✍️ Drawing Tips:

  • Use a steady hand and follow the dotted guide
  • Draw at your natural speed — don't rush or go too slow
  • For best results, complete the full spiral (at least 3 loops)
  • Try both spiral and wave patterns for a more complete analysis

🤖 The AI Models:

  • MLP (16 features) — Analyzes spatial biomarkers like stroke width, curvature, and fractal dimension
  • CNN (EfficientNet-B0+CBAM) — Analyzes the visual pattern using attention-enhanced deep learning
  • Ensemble — Combines all models with a meta-learner for the most reliable prediction

📈 Understanding Results:

  • LOW RISK (0-33%) — No significant PD indicators detected
  • MODERATE (33-66%) — Some indicators present, consider further testing
  • HIGH RISK (66-100%) — Significant indicators detected, consult a doctor
